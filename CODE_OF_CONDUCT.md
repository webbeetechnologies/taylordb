# Code of Conduct

As a contributor to TaylorDB, you are expected to follow these guidelines:

- **Be respectful**: We are a community of developers, and we expect all our members to be respectful of one another.
- **Be inclusive**: We welcome contributions from everyone, regardless of their background or experience level.
- **Be professional**: We expect all our members to conduct themselves in a professional manner.

If you witness any behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it to us immediately.
